# Cover note for outside checkers

Subject: Could you check one or two curves of {simulation}? (5 minutes each)

Hello {name},

I maintain sim-highline, an open dataset and web tool that puts galaxy scaling relations from published simulations and observations on one plot, with the definitions that decide whether two curves can be compared (https://shubhanbhatia42.github.io/sim-highline/site/index.html). Most of the simulation curves were read from published figures by me, and nobody who knows the simulations has checked them. That is the main reason the dataset cannot yet be trusted by others, and why I am writing to you.

Attached is a page with {n} curves, including some of {simulation}. For each curve it shows the plot as stored, six of its values, the paper and figure it came from, and the definitions I recorded. If you have five minutes for one of them, could you open the cited figure and tell me:

1. Is this the run and the panel that the record names?
2. Do the values match the figure to about 0.1 dex?
3. Are the definitions (stellar mass aperture, IMF, population) right?

A reply of "fine", "wrong, here is why" or "I cannot tell" is equally useful. Each curve has a link that opens a prefilled issue; email works too. I will record your answer in the project (with your name only if you say so), correct or remove any record that is wrong, and credit you in the release notes if you wish. If you would rather your simulation's curves were removed or attributed differently, say so and it will be done.

Thank you,
{maintainer}
