# Analysis: how much of the disagreement between simulated z ~ 0 stellar mass functions do documented definitions explain?

Generated by `scripts/analyze_harmonization.py` from release 0.24.0; do not edit by hand. The pre-specified protocol is [`PROTOCOL-harmonization.md`](PROTOCOL-harmonization.md) (with amendments A1 and A2, both written before any E1, E2 or E4 statistic existed); this page reports what it gave, null results included. Seed 20261014.

## Result

- **E1 - not detected.** R = 0.137 (95 % interval [-0.892, 0.512]; permutation p = 0.083). By the rule fixed in advance this is not a detection because the lower end of the bootstrap interval (-0.892) is not above 0; the permutation p-value is 0.083, not below 0.05; the sign of R differs between the primary analysis and S1 or S2.
- **E2 - upper bound.** If every suite whose aperture is undocumented (FIREbox, Horizon-AGN, Magneticum Pathfinder) took whichever aperture of the library suits it best, aperture could account for at most R = 0.144; the same search in 255 counterfactual worlds (maps mirrored or sign-flipped) gives a 95th percentile of 0.266 (maximum 0.266), so the bound does not exceed what a maximisation over meaningless maps reaches (null median 0.210).
- **E3 - universality.** total -> 30: suite dependent. Where a map is suite dependent, its between-suite spread is the uncertainty of applying it to another code. Pairs measured in fewer than three suites cannot be called universal.
- **E4 - power.** With the same suites, an injected effect of any tested size (up to twice the measured maps) is not detected in 80 % of draws; the false-positive rate with no effect is 1 %.

### What each operator did

- IMF correction alone lowers the between-suite variance (R = 0.260, 95 % interval [0.000, 0.516]).
- mass-scatter correction alone raises the between-suite variance (R = -0.326, 95 % interval [-1.129, 0.000]).
- aperture correction alone leaves unchanged the between-suite variance (R = 0.007, 95 % interval [-0.018, 0.058]).
- By level range: R = 0.309 in the body (phi from -2.0 to -3.0) and -0.351 in the tail (-3.25 to -4.5); the pre-specified test uses all levels.
- 3 of 9 suites receive an aperture correction (largest mean shift 0.015 dex); 3 already use the 30 pkpc reference and 3 do not document their aperture. The largest shift of any library map at the four probe masses is 0.131 dex, against a between-suite standard deviation of m(phi) of 0.148 dex (median over levels).
- No tested size of injected aperture effect is detected in 80 % of draws, so with these suites a non-detection of aperture effects is uninformative about whether they exist; it only bounds what the E1 rule could see.

R is the fraction of the between-suite variance of m(phi), the stellar mass at fixed abundance, that disappears when each suite's curve is expressed in the reference definitions (Chabrier IMF, no added mass scatter, 3D 30 pkpc aperture). It is a property of these suites at these levels, not of the physics: sampling noise, differences in the simulation models and everything not documented stay in the denominator.

## 6. E4: what these suites could have detected

Starting from the harmonized curves, each suite is given an aperture class from {30, 50, 100, total} and shown through the forward map of one random library suite scaled by lambda, with 0.02 dex noise in log Phi; the E1 pipeline is then run with the universal maps (100 draws, 200 bootstrap and permutation resamples each).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | 100 | -0.003 | -0.005 | 0.077 | 1 % |

| 1.0 | 100 | 0.014 | 0.010 | 0.067 | 3 % |

At lambda = 0 nothing is injected and the pipeline applies maps that are not needed, so the rate in the last column is a false-positive rate. The recovered R falling short of the true R measures the cost of applying the average map of other codes (the transfer error).

## 8. Deviations from the protocol

- An E3 was computed under the earlier level-matched design and its outcome was seen before amendment A2 replaced the aperture operator (details in section 8 of the protocol); the rule and the new result are the ones reported here.
- The E1 permutation test shuffles the complete label (IMF, aperture class, stated scatter) among all suites, including those with no measured map, rather than only among correctable suites; the null it generates is therefore somewhat wider than the protocol described.
- The Illustris 30 pkpc curve and its total curve come from different papers (Pillepich+18 and Genel+14), whereas the EAGLE and COLIBRE maps come from one figure each; the protocol listed three codes without that difference.
- E3 is evaluated in the direction in which the map is applied (to 30 pkpc); the protocol named the pair 30 pkpc to total, which is the same map in the other direction.

## 9. Limitations

- The library has three codes (Illustris/AREPO, EAGLE/GADGET-SPH, COLIBRE/SWIFT-SPH); the 30 to 50 pkpc map rests on COLIBRE alone and is applied to other codes on the assumption of transfer.
- 'Total' mass is a family (SUBFIND subhalo mass, all bound particles); the library treats it as one class.
- Curves are digitized from figures (stated uncertainty 0.01-0.02 dex); the EAGLE aperture figure's legend (10, 30, 100 pkpc) disagrees with its caption (30, 50, 100 pkpc) and the legend is used.
- Cubic remaps cannot follow a sharp bend in the true map; the accuracy on synthetic maps is stated in `scripts/test_harmonization.py`.
- Suites are not independent draws from a population of simulations; the cluster bootstrap treats them as exchangeable.
- Sampling noise in the tail is not modelled; it lowers the ceiling on R before and after harmonization alike.
- A result at z ~ 0 for the mass function says nothing about other epochs or relations, which are untested.
- Nothing here changes a stored record; harmonized curves exist only inside the analysis.

## 10. Reproduce

`python3 scripts/analyze_harmonization.py` (about a few minutes; deterministic given the seed) rewrites this file and `data/analysis/harmonization.json`; `python3 scripts/test_harmonization.py` checks the machinery on synthetic data and that these outputs are not stale.
