# Analysis: parametric versus structural uncertainty in the cosmic star formation history of simulated galaxy formation

Generated by `scripts/analyze_model_form.py` from release 0.22.0; do not edit by hand. The pre-specified protocol is [`PROTOCOL-model-form.md`](PROTOCOL-model-form.md); this page reports what it gave, null results included. Seed 20261015.

## Result

- **E1 - no funnel detected.** The spread is 0.169 dex at z = 0.5 to 3 and 0.369 dex at z = 5 to 7.5 across 7 families (phi = 0.340, 90 % interval [-0.27, 0.61]); by the rule fixed in advance this is not a detection because the lower end of the bootstrap interval (-0.272) is not above 0; the Pitman-Morgan p-value is 0.066, not below 0.05. With this many families a funnel of a factor three would be detected about two times in three (protocol, section 5).
- **E3 - reachability.** Retuning the four feedback parameters of each code within the CAMELS ranges, IllustrisTNG and SIMBA come no closer than 0.079 dex rms in the SFRD over z = 0.1 to 8.1 (emulators on random halves of the simulations, 500 per code: 5th to 95th percentile 0.052 to 0.093); the same code against its other half leaves 0.009 (median) and at most 0.012 (95th percentile), and two seeds of these two codes differ by 0.130 dex rms. At the fiducial parameters they are 0.218 dex apart. By the rule fixed in advance (amendment A1) the difference is **removable**.
- **E4 - calibrate and extrapolate.** Tuned to the same observed SFRD at z <= 3, the two codes' 90 % intervals for the response of the model at z = 4.1 to 7.7 are disjoint at 0 % of the redshifts: **parametric** disagreement after calibration. Interval widths grow with redshift by a factor 0.9 (IllustrisTNG) and 1.0 (SIMBA) from the three lowest targets (z <= 4.9) to the other seven.
- **Validations.** Not all hold: V4 SIMBA; see the validation table in section 3 and read the corresponding conclusions with that qualification.
- **What E3 and E4 can see.** On synthetic twin codes (`scripts/power_model_form.py`, `docs/POWER-model-form.md`) a verdict of structural needs a step between the codes that the four parameters cannot absorb: about 0.6 dex above z = 3.5 when the parameters move the SFRD by a few tenths of a dex, about 1 dex when they move it three times as much; smaller steps are classed removable or parametric (E4 also mixed), and identical codes never came out structural.

### What it means

- Within the CAMELS ranges one code can be retuned to reproduce the other's star formation history to within the scatter between two seeds of the same codes: the difference between these two codes is parametric.
- The four feedback parameters move the SFRD of IllustrisTNG by 0.337 dex rms at z < 3 and 0.354 dex at z > 3, and that of SIMBA by 0.220 and 0.275 dex; the seed-to-seed scatter of a 25 Mpc/h box is 0.10 (IllustrisTNG) and 0.08 (SIMBA) dex at the median redshift.

R and the other statistics are properties of these simulations at these redshifts and this resolution; sampling noise, box size and everything not modelled stay in them. Section 7 lists the limits.

## 6. Deviations from the protocol

- Sensitivity S9 (cosmology free) was run for the parametric spread and E4 only: the reachability search over six coordinates per code was dropped (amendment A1).

## 7. Limitations

- Nine families, seven balanced: a funnel of modest size would not be detected (power table in the protocol).
- The published curves differ in volume and resolution; cosmic variance and numerical effects are not removed.
- Model families share heritage (Illustris, IllustrisTNG, THESAN; EAGLE, FLAMINGO, COLIBRE); the bootstrap treats them as exchangeable.
- CAMELS boxes are 25 Mpc/h with 256^3 elements: E3 and E4 describe how the models respond at that resolution, not the absolute SFRD of the full-resolution simulations, and star formation above z about 6 is resolution-limited.
- The reachable set depends on the CAMELS parameter ranges, which are not the ranges the code authors would accept; the parameters of the two codes are not the same physical quantities.
- Two codes only: whether the IllustrisTNG-SIMBA distance is typical cannot be said.
- The observed SFRD is a fit with an assumed systematic error, not a likelihood; E4 treats the systematic as common to the eight calibration redshifts and the other errors as independent, and its power is limited (protocol section 5 and `docs/POWER-model-form.md`): steps between the codes smaller than a few tenths of a dex are classed parametric.
- A spread in dex is mechanically larger where the SFRD changes faster with redshift, so a funnel in dex does not by itself say that the models disagree more in timing or physics there; box size, resolution and the number of families that reach each redshift also change with z, and the all-family spread curve S(z) is descriptive for that reason.
- E2: variant classes are confounded with families and boxes (parametric, component and ablation variants are all IllustrisTNG; calibration and implementation variants are FLAMINGO); some variants exist in one band only; the interval ignores family clustering.
- E3: the minimiser seeks the corners of the parameter box, where an emulator trained on a Latin hypercube has the fewest simulations; the controls share that search, which keeps the comparison fair, but D is biased low by the search and high by emulator noise, and the shrunken ranges are the less extrapolated estimates. 63 % of the CAMELS grid points lie above z = 3.1.
- E5: the observed fit is converted to a Chabrier IMF by dividing by 1.6 (0.204 dex), the simulations by the website's offsets (0.235 dex from Salpeter), a difference of 0.03 dex; the observational SFRD is integrated down to a luminosity limit whereas the simulated one is a total.

## 8. Reproduce

`python3 scripts/ingest_camels_sfrd.py` rebuilds `data/ensembles/camels-sfrd.json` from the public CAMELS files (about 25 minutes; the hashes of the files read are stored) and `python3 scripts/validate_camels_closure.py` rebuilds V1; `python3 scripts/analyze_model_form.py` rewrites this file and `data/analysis/model-form.json` from them and from the committed curves; `python3 scripts/test_model_form.py` checks the machinery on synthetic ensembles and that these outputs are not stale.
