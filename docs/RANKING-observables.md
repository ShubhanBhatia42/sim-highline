## What is solid and what is not

- **Black hole occupation is far ahead of everything else** (9.5 noise units; its whole half-sample range sits above 8). Dropping it takes the all-at-once distance from 4.8 to 3.7, which is still above the margin of 1. It is also the property with the least evidence behind it: the outputs are the fraction of galaxies that have a black hole at all, which depends on the rule that plants black holes more than on the feedback dials, and there are few bins.
- **Gas metallicity is second** (1.7); the kernel-ridge emulator gives 1.8, so this one does not hinge on the polynomial form.
- **The three in the middle (star-forming main sequence, stellar metallicity, stellar-to-halo mass ratio) sit near the margin of 1** and their verdict is *unresolved* in the registered study: their ranges overlap 1. Their order among themselves should not be read.
- **Everything below that is removable alone**: the dials can bring those properties together.
- **With Astrid added** (exploratory, 97 outputs usable in all three codes): black hole occupation separates SIMBA from both others (8.9 from TNG, 2.0 from Astrid) while TNG and Astrid are 0.01 apart. So the headline property is better described as 'SIMBA is the odd one out' than as 'TNG against SIMBA'.
