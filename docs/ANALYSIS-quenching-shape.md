## Result

- **Q1 - shape alone.** The green-valley fraction and the width of the star-forming sequence (blocks: 4, outputs: 18) are **removable**: retuning within the CAMELS ranges brings the two codes within one noise unit on these outputs. Half-sample between-code distance, emulator error removed: median 0.00 noise units (5th to 95th percentile 0.00 to 0.10); same-code controls at most 0.00; margin 1. On all simulations: 0.00 corrected, 0.24 uncorrected, 0.51 at the fiducial parameters.
- **Q2 - shape beyond the usual two.** Adding the shape outputs to the quenched fraction and the star-forming main sequence **adds nothing detectable**: the rise beyond what the same-code controls show is below 0.1 noise units even at its largest. Alone, the quenched fraction and the star-forming main sequence is removable; with the shape outputs the set is removable. Excess of the change of the raw between-code distance over the mean change of the two same-code controls, split by split: median -0.163, 5th to 95th percentile -0.207 to -0.099 noise units (n = 100 splits). The sign is negative because each block has the same weight: blocks on which the two codes already agree lower the root mean distance of the set they join. That is dilution, not evidence that the shape closes the gap. The unadjusted change of the between-code distance: median -0.160 (-0.193 to -0.101); of the controls: IllustrisTNG 0.010 (-0.010 to 0.032), SIMBA 0.004 (-0.017 to 0.032).
- **Q3 - shape in the joint set.** Adding the shape outputs to the joint set of the earlier study **adds nothing detectable**: the rise beyond what the same-code controls show is below 0.1 noise units even at its largest. Alone, the joint set of the earlier study is structural; with the shape outputs the set is structural. Excess of the change of the raw between-code distance over the mean change of the two same-code controls, split by split: median -0.245, 5th to 95th percentile -0.255 to -0.221 noise units (n = 100 splits). The sign is negative because each block has the same weight: blocks on which the two codes already agree lower the root mean distance of the set they join. That is dilution, not evidence that the shape closes the gap. The unadjusted change of the between-code distance: median -0.245 (-0.252 to -0.219); of the controls: IllustrisTNG 0.001 (-0.003 to 0.008), SIMBA -0.001 (-0.004 to 0.005).
- **Validations.** Not all hold: V1, V4; read the conclusions above with the qualification below and in section 2. V1: the emulators of the shape outputs are less accurate than the seed noise (median cross-validation error 1.36 and 1.26 times the seed noise; the share within 1.5 times is 0.83 and 0.78, the rule asks for 0.8 each). The emulators' error is part of the noise scale, so it widens the unit in which distances are measured; a low distance therefore means no difference larger than that combined noise could be shown, and the power to see a small shape difference is limited. V4: the point distance of the shape set changes from 0.328 with 4 random starts to 0.213 with 8 (54 %). Both are far below the margin of 1: when a distance is this small the surface the search minimises is flat and noisy, and different starts end in different places. A larger number of starts can only lower a found minimum, so the verdict removable does not depend on it; the check after the run (`docs/CHECKS-quenching-shape.md`) tests this with up to 64 starts.

Distances are in units of the noise scale of each output (the standard deviation of the difference between one run of each code plus the emulators' error); the margin is 1. Everything is for CAMELS resolution (25 Mpc/h, 256^3), instantaneous star formation rates of Subfind subhaloes, and the four astrophysical parameters per code that CAMELS varies.

## 8. Deviations from the protocol and implementation choices

- Emulator degrees were chosen on all eligible outputs of the earlier study together with the eligible shape outputs (protocol section 4 says "degree chosen by cross-validation" without naming the outputs): IllustrisTNG 3, SIMBA 3; the earlier study chose 3 and 3.
- Sensitivity S3 (lower threshold) was computed from the same arrays during the same ingest (family `gvwide`), not by a second ingest, and not used to choose anything.
- Sensitivity S1 removes the two lowest mass bins from every mass-binned family in the sets it is applied to (the same rule as the earlier study's S8), not only from the shape families.
- 100 random splits per set, as the protocol states; the earlier study used 200 for its joint set. The split streams are the earlier study's (same seed, as the estimators are reused unchanged).
- Two simulations per code and set (LH_0 and CV_0, the first snapshot) were printed when the ingest was tested; no distance, emulator or verdict was computed before this run.
- The wording of the report was revised after the run (clearer sentences, an explanation of the negative excess and of the two validations that do not hold); no number was recomputed, and the first output is in the git history.

## 9. Limitations

Those of the protocol (section 9) apply and are not repeated in full here: two codes at CAMELS resolution; instantaneous star formation rates and zero-SFR galaxies counted as quenched; the choice of two summaries of a distribution that has more; estimators calibrated on a different block layout. A result of "adds nothing detectable" means that this method, with these 25 Mpc/h boxes and these two summaries, did not detect more separation, not that none exists.

## 10. Reproduce

`python3 scripts/ingest_camels_distshape.py` (about 10 minutes, needs fsspec and aiohttp) rebuilds `data/ensembles/camels-distshape.json`; `python3 scripts/analyze_quenching_shape.py` rewrites this file and `data/analysis/quenching-shape.json`; `python3 scripts/test_quenching_shape.py` checks the machinery on synthetic worlds and that these outputs are not stale.
