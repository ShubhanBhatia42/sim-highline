# Expert audit request

sim-highline is an open, definition-aware dataset of galaxy scaling relations from simulations and observations. Nobody who knows these simulations has checked it yet; the checks so far are the maintainer's and scripts'. This packet is for the person who agrees to spend ten minutes on one suite.

## What to ask one person per suite

> Could you check two records of {simulation} in sim-highline? Each takes about five minutes: open the cited figure or table, and tell me whether (1) the curve is the one named (run, population, panel), (2) the axis values at two labelled ticks match, (3) the definitions block is right (mass aperture, IMF, SFR timescale, halo definition), and (4) the epoch is right. A one-line reply per record is enough: "ok", or what to change.

The records are listed in `docs/expert-audit-sample.md` (two per simulation, deterministic, regenerated by `node scripts/make-audit-sample.mjs`). A reviewer who prefers a different curve can pick any record from the data explorer (`dist/highline.html`, click a curve, then Evidence).

## What to send

- The two record ids and their links from `docs/expert-audit-sample.md`.
- The one-page summary (`site/about.html`) and the data card (`docs/DATA-CARD.md`).
- A statement of how the answer will be used: recorded in `data/audit-outcomes.json` with the reviewer's name only if they agree, and the record corrected or removed if wrong.

## Recording outcomes

Append to `data/audit-outcomes.json` (`record`, `reviewer` or "anonymous", `date`, `verdict` of ok / corrected / removed, `note`). Release notes should state how many sample records have been externally checked and how many were corrected.

## Suggested suites to start with

The suites with most digitized records are the ones most likely to hide a wrong panel or population: FLAMINGO, NewHorizon, Illustris and IllustrisTNG, EAGLE, Horizon-AGN. `docs/author-requests.md` lists the same papers as candidates for replacing digitized curves with the authors' tables, so one email can carry both asks.
