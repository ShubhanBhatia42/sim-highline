## Changelog bullets (task darwin-catalogue)

- DARWIN-1: two more relations counted from the project's public catalogues, 23 records (`scripts/ingest_darwin1_catalogue.py --relations` for the counts in `data/ensembles/darwin1-shmr-bh.json`, `--records` for `data/curves/darwin-catalogue.json`, tier catalog-derived). (1) The stellar-to-halo mass ratio of central galaxies, 11 records, z = 0.19 to 10. The catalogue has Friends-of-Friends group masses (`halo/mtot`) and no M200, so the halo mass is recorded as its own definition (`FoF-total-mass-all-species`) and the comparability rules do not score it against M200-based or any other halo-mass definition (checked on the JavaScript side in `scripts/test-compare.mjs` and on the Python side in `scripts/test_rules_parity.py`). The catalogue has no central flag: the central is the galaxy of largest stellar mass of the halo, the rule the finder's own paper (Kim et al. 2023) uses; taking the largest total mass instead moves no bin median by more than 0.034 dex. Only halo-mass bins in which every FoF halo hosts a galaxy are kept. (2) Black hole mass against stellar mass, 12 records, z = 0.19 to 10 (with the z = 2.09 snapshot for the paper's z = 2.1 panel): every galaxy with 100 or more star particles and at least one black hole particle, centrals and satellites, black hole mass the sum over its black hole particles (`sub/mMBH`); galaxies without a black hole particle are not in the relation.
- Compared with the paper's raster Figs. 10 and 11 (internal check only; the figures are not digitized, the axes are calibrated from their tick marks, `scripts/darwin_raster_check.py`): the paper's median stellar-to-halo ratio is 0.07 to 0.17 dex higher than the catalogue curve at z = 3 (5 bins) and 0.03 to 0.08 dex higher at z = 0.19 (8 bins), the sign expected from its M200,c being lower than the FoF mass (cause not tested). The catalogue galaxies drawn as dots on the black hole figure reproduce its grey dots: the 50th percentile of the dot pixels agrees within 0.1 dex in 10 of 12 stellar-mass bins at z = 2.1 and in 11 of 13 at z = 0.19 (largest difference 0.28 and 0.10 dex); galaxies with exactly one black hole particle cannot cover the figure above about 10^10 Msun. Whether the paper sums the black hole particles or takes the most massive one is not stated and cannot be separated with the catalogue.
- DARWIN-1 sizes, gas-phase metallicities and stellar metallicities cannot be derived from the public catalogues: the 39 datasets of each of 12 snapshots checked hold counts, masses, positions and velocities of the dark matter, gas, star and black hole particles and the halo links only, with no radius, abundance, metal mass, star formation rate or M200 (`data/sources-catalogue.json`, entry `darwin1-catalogs`).
- The Explorer page grows by about 10 kB compressed (801 to 810 kB); `scripts/test-performance.mjs` budgets 800 kB and fails at 809 kB (it was at 799 kB before these records, as the open decision notes; measured here: 809 kB now, gzip of the built page 801.0 kB before and 810.5 kB after). Raise the budget or trim records: a maintainer decision.
