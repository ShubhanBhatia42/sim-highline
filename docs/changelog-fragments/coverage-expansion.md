Changelog bullets (Unreleased):

- New simulation: CoDa II (Ocvirk et al. 2020, RAMSES-CUDATON radiation hydrodynamics on a fixed 4096^3 grid, a 94.44 Mpc constrained realization of the Local Universe), 12 records: the intrinsic UV luminosity functions at z = 6, 7, 8 and 10 transcribed from the paper's appendix table (median of five sub-boxes with their minimum-maximum envelope; 49 checks), and the galaxy stellar mass function and the stellar mass-halo mass relation at the same four epochs counted from the public VizieR catalogue VI/146 (28 checks, among them the shares of the star formation rate in galaxies brighter than M_AB1600 = -17 and -13 that the paper prints: 63.7 and 95.7 percent against 63 and 94 at z = 6, 18.3 and 75.1 against 18 and 76 at z = 10). The paper's table lies 0.044 dex above a count of its own public catalogue at the bright end and up to 0.5 dex above it at the faint end; this is not reconciled and is stated in the records' notes. Internal check only.
- New simulation: The Three Hundred (Cui et al. 2018, 324 re-simulated cluster regions with two SPH codes, GADGET-MUSIC and GADGET-X), 6 records for two relations that had observations only: the gas fraction inside R500 (read from Fig. 5) and the Y500-M500 relation (read from Fig. 10, with the two power-law fits of Table 7 stored as published fits). Axes are checked against the drawn Planck 2014 line and the two fit lines (within 0.007 dex, which also fixes the h = 0.678 conversion), the 5/3 self-similar slope, the paper's completeness limit M500 = 4.60e14 h^-1 Msun and the cosmic baryon fraction (24 checks). The data are available only on request. Not read: the stellar fraction, the central stellar-to-halo mass relation (nothing printed to check the axes against) and the satellite mass function (counts per cluster, not a density). Internal check only.
- FLAMINGO: 6 records from Kugel et al. 2023 (the calibration paper): the best-fitting calibration simulations at three resolutions ((100, 200, 400 Mpc)^3 boxes), the z = 0 stellar mass function and the z = 0.1 gas fraction inside R500c, which FLAMINGO had no records for. Each of the six panels is checked against numbers the paper prints (10-particle lines against the particle masses, the fitted mass ranges, the seven X-ray gas fractions of Table 5 drawn in the figure, the stated binning; 24 checks). These are the calibration targets, not predictions, and the simulated stellar masses carry the 0.07 dex random error the paper adds before counting. Internal check only.
- The palette gives a family a fourth lightness step (a family had at most three members), so that CoDa II joins the THESAN family and The Three Hundred the Magneticum family.

Papers looked at and not ingested (reason in one line each; none was read in full):

- Obelisk (2002.04045): vector figures for the mass function, UV luminosity function, SFR density, stellar-to-halo mass and black hole mass at one protocluster zoom; step-function histograms, a units inconsistency between text and axis (h^3 cMpc^-3 against cMpc^-3) and no printed number to check the axes against beyond "about 1e-2".
- FirstLight (2404.02537): mass and UV luminosity functions are drawn as Poisson bands with no central curve; the integrated efficiency is normalised by a baryon fraction whose value the paper does not give.
- CROC I (1403.4245): UV luminosity functions at six redshifts in one panel, each shifted by 1 dex, with no table and no printed value to check against.
- Renaissance (1503.01110), CoDa I (1511.00011), Horizon Run 5 (2408.07258), FLARES I (2004.07283, the main figures): raster images.
- Aurora (1603.00034): vector figures but their tick labels are outlined paths (no text layer) and the paper has no table; not read.
- Hydrangea (1703.10610): the mass functions are those of cluster satellites, several normalised by cluster mass, which is not a number density; not read.
- Dianoga (2006.13951): vector figures of the z = 0 mass function, the z about 2 main sequence and gas fractions exist; not read within the three-paper cap, a candidate for a later round.
- HESTIA (2008.04926): outlined tick labels; the table of z = 0 Local Group properties could give a stellar-to-halo mass table; not read.
- LYRA III and LYRA (Sureda) (2209.03366, 2511.10582), VELA (2210.15372), EDGE (1909.04664, 2004.09530), VINTERGATAN III (2006.06012): a handful of individual simulated galaxies each (VELA a larger set of zoom-ins); their tables of z = 0 properties could give stellar-to-halo mass and size points; not read.
- Romulus25 (Jung et al. 2022, 2203.00016): vector stellar-to-halo mass figure of group centrals; not read, a candidate.
- THESAN data release and companions (2309.06475, 2110.01628, 2110.02966): mostly data-release and intergalactic-medium content; the THESAN-1 relations they show are already in the dataset, and the THESAN-2 against TNG comparison figures of 2110.01628 were not read.
