- New relation: emission-line luminosity function (`elf`; 17 records, H-alpha only so far). Observations: Sobral et al. 2013 (HiZELS, z = 0.40, 0.84, 1.47, 2.23; corrected for [NII] and for 1 mag of extinction) and Covelo-Paz et al. 2025 (JWST NIRCam grism, observed, z about 4.45, 5.3 and 6.15), both from the tables in the papers' LaTeX sources. Simulation: SPHINX20, intrinsic and dust-attenuated functions at seven snapshots (z = 4.64 to 10), computed from the public data release and checked against the function the paper plots (0.015 dex rms over 250 plotted bins). The line (H-alpha alone or blended with [NII]) and the dust treatment (intrinsic or attenuated) are hard comparability keys, so an observed function is never silently compared with an intrinsic one; the aperture, cosmology, population and dust method give caveats. Internal checks only; no outside expert has reviewed these records.
