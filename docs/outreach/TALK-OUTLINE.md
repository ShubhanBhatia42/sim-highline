# Five-slide talk outline

Generated by `scripts/make-outreach.mjs` from dataset v0.24.0. **Nothing here has been sent.** Ten minutes plus questions, for a research group.

1. **The problem.** Two curves from two simulations differ. Is it physics or definitions? Show one real pair (a stellar mass function from the Explorer with the comparable / caveats / not comparable marks). Say: only 4% of 2,174 cross-simulation curve pairs are measured alike on what the sources document.
2. **The tool.** Live demo, 90 seconds: look up a relation at z about 8; paste a curve; read the offsets table. The data: 1,736 curves, 19 simulations, 89 observational and empirical sources; what "digitized from a figure" means and why those are never ranked.
3. **The question it made possible.** Turn the four dials of TNG and SIMBA: can one be made to look like the other? Demo "Make SIMBA look like TNG" on the stellar mass function (it works) and then show what it breaks elsewhere.
4. **The result.** All at once: 4.8 noise units apart after the best retuning; 2 properties that stay apart at every dial setting tested (black hole occupation, gas metallicity), 6 that can be matched. Show the bar chart. Say what was written before the result and what was checked after (robustness: a second implementation, another emulator, other weightings: 3.8 to 5.4; a third code: SIMBA stands apart on black hole occupation).
5. **Limits and ask.** CAMELS resolution (without the two lowest mass bins 4.8 becomes 2.5); two codes; the digitized fraction; unreviewed. Ask: where would you use it, what is missing, which author's table should replace which figure reading. Put the address and the feedback link on the last slide.

Have ready: the data-quality page (how far to trust it), `docs/SCEPTIC.md` (the objections and answers), and the audit packet if the question is about extraction accuracy.
