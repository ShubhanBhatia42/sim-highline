# Pilot script for three testers

Generated by `scripts/make-outreach.mjs` from dataset v0.23.0. **Nothing here has been sent.** Sit with each person (or a call with their screen shared), say nothing about the site beyond the first line, and write down what happens. About 25 minutes each.

## Before

- Pick three people who have not seen it: one who **runs simulations**, one who **compares simulations with observations**, one who is **learning galaxy formation** (a student). Do not use anyone who has watched you build it.
- Say: "This is a tool I made. I am testing the tool, not you. Please think aloud. I will not help unless you ask me to give up."
- Give them the address https://shubhanbhatia42.github.io/sim-highline/ and nothing else. Start a timer.

## Tasks (read one at a time, do not show the list)

| # | for | task | done when | what to write down |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | everyone | "Find out what this is for." | they say it in their own words | the time, the first thing they clicked, the words they used |
| 2 | observer | "What do simulations predict for the stellar mass function at redshift 8?" | they reach a plot with several simulations at z about 8 | the clicks, whether they found the redshift control, whether they noticed the legend badges |
| 3 | simulation runner | "Here is a curve from your own run (give them the example file or their own). Is it consistent with what has been published?" | they have offsets against published curves and say which are fair to compare | where they hesitated, whether they understood the three marks (comparable, caveats, not comparable) |
| 4 | student | "If supernova winds were twice as strong in both universes, what would change?" | they use the dials and say what moved | whether they understood the sentence at the top, the panel colours, the shading |
| 5 | everyone | "You want to cite this in a paper. What do you do?" | they find the citation and the BibTeX | time, hesitations |
| 6 | everyone | "Is there anything here you do not trust? Why?" | they answer | the exact words; whether they found the data-quality page |

## After (ask each, write the answer word for word)

1. "What would you use this for, if anything?"
2. "What was confusing? Which word did you not know?"
3. "What did you expect to find that was not there?"
4. "Would you tell a colleague about it? What would you say?"
5. "On a scale of 1 to 5, how likely are you to come back in the next month? Why that number?"

## What to record in the log (`docs/outreach/pilot-log.md`, create it when the first session is done)

For every session: date, role, time to say what it is for, tasks completed unaided, tasks where you had to help, every word they did not understand, every thing they asked for that does not exist, and the answers above. **Do not summarise in your own words until all three are done.**

## How to read the result

- If two of three could not complete task 1 within two minutes, the front page is the problem, not the testers.
- If nobody would come back, say so in the retrospective (task 10.06) and apply the stopping rule in `docs/PROJECT.md`.
- A request that two people make independently outranks anything on the current roadmap.
