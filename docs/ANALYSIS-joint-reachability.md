# Analysis: can retuning make two feedback models agree on every galaxy observable at once?

Generated by `scripts/analyze_joint_reachability.py` from release 0.22.0; do not edit by hand. ## Result

- **J1 - joint.** With one parameter pair per code, IllustrisTNG and SIMBA stay 4.77 noise units apart over 169 outputs in 35 blocks (11 families at z = 0.0, 0.95, 2.15, 3.0, 4.0): the half-sample median of the distance with the emulators' error at the minimiser removed (5th to 95th percentile 4.64 to 4.86; uncorrected median 4.84); on all simulations 4.82 corrected, 4.85 uncorrected, 6.35 at the fiducial parameters. Same-code controls: at most 0.00 corrected. A distance of 1 is the standard deviation of the difference between one run of each code. With the search range cut to 75 % of the half-width the joint verdict is structural. By the rule fixed in advance (amendment A1) the joint verdict is **structural**. Largest shares of the squared distance: bhocc at z = 2.15 (28 %); mzr at z = 2.15 (13 %); bhocc at z = 0.95 (10 %).
- **J2 - each alone.** Fitted alone, 2 of 11 families are structural: black hole occupation, gas metallicity at fixed M*; 6 are removable: stellar mass function, quenched fraction, black hole mass at fixed M*, stellar half-mass radius at fixed M*, gas fraction at fixed M*, stellar mass and SFR density; 3 are unresolved: star-forming main sequence, stellar metallicity at fixed M*, stellar-to-halo mass ratio.
- **J3 - the cost of agreeing.** With one parameter pair for everything the squared distance is 2.82 times what it would be if every family were tuned separately (half-sample 5th to 95th 2.60 to 3.08); for two halves of one code, which is what noise and the extra parameters alone give, the same ratio is 2.04 (95th percentile 2.74). The structural joint distance is classed **irreducible within the CAMELS ranges** (at least one family is structural even alone).
- **J4 - what separates them.** In forward selection the families come in the order bhocc, mzr, size, bhms, fgas, shmr, zstar, sfms, qf, gsmf, rho; the first prefix that is structural has 1 family (bhocc, D = 9.49). That is the same as J2 for that family.
- **Validations.** All five hold.

Distances are in units of the noise scale of each output (the standard deviation of the difference between one run of each code plus the emulators' error); the margin is 1. Distances marked corrected have the emulators' variance at the minimiser removed in quadrature (A1). Everything is for CAMELS resolution (25 Mpc/h, 256^3).

## 9. Limitations

- Two codes; CAMELS resolution; the reachable set depends on the CAMELS parameter ranges, not the ranges the code authors would accept. The minimiser sits at the edge of the parameter box where the emulators are least constrained: the emulators' variance at the minimiser is estimated per split and removed (A1), and the verdicts at 75 % of the range are reported.
- The distance depends on the choice of outputs and their weights (equal weight per block, so blocks with few outputs weigh more per output); the margin of 1 is the scatter between two seeds, a unit and not a physical tolerance. D is the root of a mean over blocks: one block far off can leave a small joint distance, which is why the shares of the squared distance are listed.
- Medians of small bins are noisy; the noise scale does not include their non-Gaussian part, is measured at the fiducial parameters only and comes from 27 seeds. The order of the forward selection is chosen on all simulations, so the verdicts of nested sets are descriptive.
- Both catalogues come from the same Subfind code and field list (checked on LH_0), but the codes differ in more than subgrid form: hydro solver, star formation algorithm, yields, wind treatment (TNG wind particles are inside the typed masses, SIMBA has none). Black hole occupation and mass compare seeding prescriptions (TNG 8e5/h Msun seeds, SIMBA much lighter), a model choice that no feedback dial changes.
- Subfind subhaloes are not galaxies seen on the sky: no aperture, selection or survey effect is modelled.

## 10. Reproduce

`python3 scripts/ingest_camels_galaxies.py` (about 35 minutes, needs fsspec and aiohttp) rebuilds `data/ensembles/camels-galaxies.json`; `python3 scripts/analyze_joint_reachability.py` rewrites this file and `data/analysis/joint-reachability.json`; `python3 scripts/test_joint_reachability.py` checks the machinery on synthetic worlds and that these outputs are not stale.
