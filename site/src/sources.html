Simulations and observations

The {{suites}} simulations differ in numerical method, code, resolution and purpose; the {{obsSources}} observational and empirical sources differ in survey, facility and what they measure. Those differences are a large part of why curves disagree, so they are recorded here.

§1Simulations

Grouped by how the gas is evolved. Dates are the year the flagship paper was posted to arXiv. Set-up, physics and purpose follow the flagship abstracts; code names, box sizes and team names that an abstract does not state come from background knowledge and are not all checked against the full papers, so check the linked papers before quoting them. The “In sim-highline” line under each entry is computed from the data.

§4What is not here yet

A search of the simulation literature (specialised dwarf-galaxy suites such as LYRA and EDGE, zoom suites such as Auriga and APOSTLE, reionization suites, cluster suites, and the 2026 COLIBRE papers) is in docs/literature-candidates.md; entries are marked ingested, queued or skipped (with the reason) in data/literature-backlog.json.
