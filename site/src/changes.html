The last releases, in plain language. Developer notes are in the changelog.

§1Releases

0.23.0 · 16 Oct 2026
The dials page: turn the four feedback dials of two simulated universes and read in a sentence what agrees and what refuses; match one to the other and see what moved. Check your own run opens from a link, has an example and states the range of offsets against comparable curves. A new study asks whether the two universes can agree on every galaxy property at once (they cannot), with the gaps in dex and fractions. New front page, glossary and FAQ.
0.22.0 · 14 Oct 2026
A study of what retuning can change in the cosmic star formation history: published simulations show no detectable growing disagreement at early times, and retuning four feedback dials brings the two codes within the scatter between seeds. New data: IllustrisTNG model variants and FLAMINGO, and the star formation history of 2,054 CAMELS simulations.
0.21.0 · 14 Oct 2026
A pre-specified test of whether matching definitions (IMF, scatter, aperture) closes the gap between simulated stellar mass functions: not detected, and the test could not have seen an effect of the measured size. New within-run aperture curves for EAGLE and COLIBRE.
0.20.0 · 13 Oct 2026
NewHorizon stellar mass functions at four epochs; new curves for COLIBRE, FIREbox, FIRE-2 and NIHAO; an automatic screen that compares every curve with the median of the others.
