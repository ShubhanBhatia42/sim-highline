Limits in brief. Two codes at CAMELS resolution, instantaneous star formation rates of Subfind subhaloes, two summaries of a distribution that has more, and estimators that were calibrated on a different layout of outputs. Internal: no outside expert has reviewed this study.

§1Question

The joint study asked whether retuning four feedback parameters can make IllustrisTNG and SIMBA agree on every galaxy observable at once. Its star-formation outputs were two numbers per mass bin: the quenched fraction and the median specific star formation rate of the galaxies that are still forming stars. A distribution has more structure than that: how many galaxies are in between the blue cloud and the red sequence (the green valley), and how wide the star-forming sequence is.

Two new outputs per stellar-mass bin: the green-valley fraction (the share of galaxies with specific star formation rate between 10−11 and 10−10.5 per year) and the width of the star-forming sequence (the 84th minus the 16th percentile of log specific star formation rate among the galaxies above 10−10.5 per year). Three questions with rules fixed in advance: is the distance between the codes for these outputs alone structural, removable or neither (Q1); do they add separation to the quenched fraction and the main sequence (Q2); do they add separation to the whole joint set (Q3)?

§2Data

Registration

The protocol was committed before the data were ingested. One amendment was made, before any distance, emulator or verdict existed for the Latin-hypercube simulations: a test on synthetic worlds showed that the first rule for Q2 and Q3 would call two identical codes different, because adding outputs raises a minimised distance a little even when there is nothing to find. The amended rule compares each change with the same-code controls of the same split. The commits are listed on the Studies page.

Simulations
The {{camelsN}} CAMELS simulations of the earlier studies (IllustrisTNG and SIMBA; 1000 Latin-hypercube simulations and 27 seeds at the fiducial parameters each), snapshots at z = 0, 0.95 and 2.15.
Outputs
Ten bins of 0.25 dex in stellar mass from 109 M☉, read from three arrays of each Subfind catalogue by range requests (data/ensembles/camels-distshape.json, scripts/ingest_camels_distshape.py); a bin needs at least 10 galaxies. {{qsEligible}}
Method
The estimators of the joint study, unchanged: polynomial ridge emulators per code, the noise left when everything else is fixed, the distance minimised over both codes' four parameters, 100 random half-sample splits per set and the margin of one noise unit.

§5Limits

  • Two codes, CAMELS resolution. 25 Mpc/h boxes with 256³ elements: the lowest mass bins hold galaxies of a few tens of star particles, where a green-valley share can be driven by single events. The validation table reports how many outputs rest on few galaxies.
  • Instantaneous rates, zero counted as quenched. A galaxy with no star formation at the snapshot is counted as quenched, so the quenched and green-valley shares both depend on how often a code assigns exactly zero.
  • Two summaries of a distribution that has more. The position of the red peak or the colour is a different study.
  • Estimators not calibrated for this layout. They were calibrated for the joint study's outputs; a result of "adds nothing detectable" means this method did not detect more separation, not that there is none.
  • Subfind subhaloes are not galaxies on the sky. No aperture, selection or survey effect is modelled.
