§1Question

Study S3 found that the cosmic star formation history alone cannot separate IllustrisTNG from SIMBA: retuning four feedback parameters brings the two within the scatter between seeds. One observable constrains few combinations of four parameters. Real calibrations use many observables at once, and two models that agree on one can disagree on another.

For a code, the reachable set is every galaxy-population summary its four parameters can produce. The structural distance between two codes is the distance between their reachable sets, in units of the noise that is left when everything else is fixed (1 is the standard deviation of the difference between one run of each). Both codes were run in the same boxes at the same resolution and are read from catalogues made by the same Subfind code with the same field definitions, so no aperture, IMF or resolution difference enters. What remains is the difference between the codes: their subgrid models, and also their hydrodynamic solvers, star formation algorithms, yields and wind treatment.

Five questions, each with a rule fixed in advance: is the distance for all observables with one parameter pair per code structural (J1); which observables are structural even when fitted alone (J2); how much does insisting on one parameter pair cost (J3); which observables carry the separation (J4); and does it change with redshift (J5).

§2Data

Registration

The protocol, the analysis code, and its calibration on synthetic worlds were committed before the real outcomes existed; an independent review preceded the run. Only the eligibility of the outputs had been looked at (section 8).

Simulations
{{camelsN}} CAMELS simulations in 25 Mpc/h boxes (256³ dark matter particles plus 256³ gas elements): per code, {{camelsLH}} Latin-hypercube simulations (four feedback factors and two cosmological parameters, a different seed each) and {{camelsCV}} seeds at the fiducial parameters. The feedback factors of the two codes are not the same physical quantities.
Observables
Binned summaries of each simulation's Subfind catalogue at z = 0, 0.95, 2.15, 3 and 4 (data/ensembles/camels-galaxies.json): the stellar mass function, quenched fraction, specific SFR of star-forming galaxies, black hole mass and occupation, gas and stellar metallicity, half-mass radius and gas fraction in bins of 0.25 dex in stellar mass from 109 M☉, the stellar-to-halo mass ratio of centrals, the halo mass function (a negative control) and the stellar mass and SFR densities. {{jrElig}}; few bins survive at z ≥ 3.
Noise
The standard deviation of the 27 cosmic-variance seeds of each code plus the emulators' cross-validation error ({{jrNoise}}); every output is divided by it, and every block of outputs gets the same weight.

§5Limits

  • Two codes, and more than subgrid form. The result is about this pair, not about structural uncertainty in general; what separates them includes the hydrodynamic solver, the star formation algorithm and the yields, not only the feedback prescriptions. Black hole occupation and mass compare seeding prescriptions (a discrete model choice that no dial changes), so a structural verdict there is expected by construction; the sensitivity analyses give the result without them.
  • CAMELS resolution. 25 Mpc/h boxes, 256³ elements: few galaxies above 1011 M☉, a mass limit of 109 M☉, almost nothing at z ≥ 3; star formation and black hole growth depend on resolution in both codes.
  • The reachable set depends on the prior, and the minimiser sits at its edge. The four factors span the ranges the CAMELS team chose, not the ranges the code authors would accept; the search reaches the corners of the design, where the emulators are least constrained and noisier than their cross-validation error says. The distances are therefore corrected for the emulators' variance at the minimiser (estimated in every split), and the verdicts with the range cut to 75 % are reported.
  • The distance depends on the observables and weights. Equal weight per block; the margin of 1 is the scatter between two seeds, a unit and not a physical tolerance. Per-family distances are in the table so that other weights can be read off.
  • Subfind subhaloes are not galaxies on the sky. No aperture, selection or survey effect is modelled; that is what makes the comparison clean and also what keeps it from being an observational test.
