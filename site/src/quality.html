How far to trust the data

Everything here is computed from the records, not typed. The short version: the curves are traceable to their sources, but most of the simulation curves were read from figures and almost none has an independent check.

§2Independent checks

A figure-reading can be checked directly only where the same simulation, relation and epoch also exists as a table or fit with identical definitions. That holds for {{qExtractN}} digitized curves ({{qExtractShare}}%). For every other digitized curve the reading has no independent check in the data; the extraction code is tested on synthetic figures with known answers, which checks the method and not each figure. {{qExtractSummary}}

§3Where the evidence is thin
