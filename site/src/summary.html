sim-highline on one page

Put your simulated galaxy curve next to every published simulation and observation, and find out which comparisons are fair. Free, open, runs in your browser. {{siteUrl}}

What you can do

Look up a relation
{{records}} curves from {{suites}} simulations and {{obsSources}} observational and empirical sources on one plot, each with its source and its definitions.
Check your own run
Paste your curve; get offsets against the published curves it can fairly be compared with. Nothing leaves your browser.
Turn the physics dials
Four feedback dials each for IllustrisTNG and SIMBA, from {{camelsN}} CAMELS simulations: what moves, what stays apart.
Take the data
CSV, JSON, BibTeX and a Python loader. CC BY 4.0.

How far to trust it

  • Not peer reviewed; built by one maintainer with an AI assistant (see Methods). {{digitizedShare}} of simulation curves were read from published figures and are never used to rank anything; where authors publish a table, use theirs.
  • The dials results are for two codes at CAMELS resolution, not for simulations in general.
  • Nobody outside the project is known to have used it yet. Used it? Say so.
