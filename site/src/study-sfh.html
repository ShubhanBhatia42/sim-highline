Abstract

Simulations are tuned to a few observations at redshift about 0 and then asked what happens elsewhere. We separate what a model can still do by retuning its own parameters from what its form forces whatever the tuning, for the cosmic star formation history. Published curves of {{mfNFamAll}} model families do not show a detectable funnel of growing disagreement (across the {{mfNFam}} families defined in both redshift bands, the spread is {{mfSdLow}} dex at z = 0.5 to 3 and {{mfSdHigh}} dex at z = 5 to 7.5: {{mfSuccess}}). In {{camelsN}} CAMELS simulations, retuning four feedback parameters per code brings IllustrisTNG and SIMBA from {{mfDfid}} to {{mfD}} dex rms ({{mfReach}}).

§1Question

Two things limit a simulation’s predictions away from the observations it was tuned to: what the model can still do by retuning its own parameters (parametric), and what its form forces whatever the tuning (structural). Climate science separates them with perturbed-physics and multi-model ensembles. Here the cosmic star formation history is the test case.

Four questions, each with a rule fixed in advance: do published simulations disagree more in the early Universe (E1); how big are the authors’ own changes (E2); can retuning remove the difference between two codes (E3); and tuned to the same observed history, do their predictions beyond it stay apart (E4)?

Registration

The protocol was written before any of these numbers existed and was amended once, before the run, after an internal review (not by an outside expert). Amendment A1 and what had been seen are in PROTOCOL-model-form.md.

Published
Curves of the cosmic star formation rate density of {{mfNFamAll}} model families, and the authors’ own resolution, parameter and cosmology variants (data/model-variants.json).
CAMELS
{{camelsN}} simulations in {{camelsBox}} Mpc/h boxes with the IllustrisTNG and SIMBA codes at identical resolution: per code, {{camelsLH}} Latin-hypercube simulations (six parameters: Ωm, σ8 and four feedback factors, a different seed each) and {{camelsCV}} seeds at the fiducial parameters. Star formation logs reduced to {{camelsBins}} redshift bins (data/ensembles/camels-sfrd.json).

§4Limits

  • Validations. {{mfVal}}
  • Scope. Two codes, one observable and CAMELS resolution are all this can speak to, and the parameters of the two codes are not the same physical quantities.
  • What the structural verdicts can see. Calibrated on synthetic twin codes, they need a step between the codes that the four parameters cannot absorb: about 0.6 dex above z = 3.5 for weak parameter responses and about 1 dex for strong ones. Smaller steps are classed removable or parametric. See POWER-model-form.md.
  • Resolution and code are confounded in the published curves. The sizes of the authors’ own variants are their choices, so only the order of magnitude of E2 carries information.

The validations, the sensitivity analyses and the full limits are in ANALYSIS-model-form.md; the pre-registration and its amendment are PROTOCOL-model-form.md.
