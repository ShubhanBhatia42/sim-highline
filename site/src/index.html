§1Start here

Turn the physics dials
Two simulated universes, IllustrisTNG and SIMBA, each with four feedback dials. Turn them and watch galaxies reshape; ask whether one universe can be made to look like the other, and see which properties stay apart.

Pick a relation and an epoch: the published simulations and observations on one plot, each labelled with where it came from (official table, paper table, fit or digitized figure) and whether its definitions allow it to be compared with the others.

How far to trust it. Not reviewed by any outside expert, and made with an AI assistant (see Methods): {{digitizedShare}} of simulation curves are read from published figures by script and are never used for ranking. Where an author or collaboration releases a table, use that. This is not a leaderboard. Methods, limits; to report an error or ask for a takedown, open an issue on the repository.

§3What it has shown

The dataset and the CAMELS ensemble ({{camelsN}} simulations of two feedback models) were used to ask four questions, each with the decision rule written down before the outcome was computed where the study is marked pre-registered. Three of the four results are null or qualified, and the pages say so. All studies and how they are registered.
