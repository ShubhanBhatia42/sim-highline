sim-highline

Put your simulated galaxy relation next to every published simulation and observation, and find out which of those comparisons are legitimate. Two curves that look different may measure different things: a different stellar mass aperture, IMF or population cut. Every curve here carries the definitions that decide it.

§1Start here

Turn the physics dials
Two simulated universes, IllustrisTNG and SIMBA, each with four feedback dials. Turn them and watch galaxies reshape; ask whether one universe can be made to look like the other, and see which properties refuse.

§2What is in it

{{dataTableCompact}}

Simulations and observations count distinct sources; records count curves. Rankable: definitions and sampling allow the curve to be used for ranking. The full table, evidence tiers, files and licences are on the Data page.

§3What it has shown

The dataset and the CAMELS ensemble ({{camelsN}} simulations of two feedback models) were used to ask four questions, each with the decision rule written down before the outcome was computed where the study is marked pre-registered. Three of the four results are null or qualified, and the pages say so. All studies and how they are registered.
