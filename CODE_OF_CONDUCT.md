# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, focus on the science and the data, and do not harass anyone. Corrections to the dataset, including by the authors of the underlying papers, are welcome and are treated as a contribution, not a criticism.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported to the maintainer through the contact address in `CITATION.cff`. The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
